A pregnancy tracker that keeps your data yours

Your pregnancy is some of the most personal information you'll ever record. It shouldn't require handing it to a company first.

Why pregnancy privacy matters more than ever

The data in a pregnancy app can reveal your health conditions, your due date, a previous loss, fertility treatment, and the exact timeline of your pregnancy. That's deeply sensitive — and in many apps it's collected behind a mandatory account, stored on servers, and sometimes shared with advertisers or analytics partners.

You shouldn't have to read a privacy policy with a law degree to track your own baby. The simplest protection is structural: if the data never leaves your device, there's nothing on a server to breach, subpoena, or sell.

What "no account" actually buys you

  • Nothing to sign up for — open the app and start. No email, no password, no profile.
  • On-device by default — your logs live on your iPhone, not in a stranger's database.
  • Private sync, your devices only — Bloom can keep your iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch in step through your own private iCloud, without a Bloom account in the middle.
  • Less to leak — no central account means no central honeypot.

Questions to ask any pregnancy app

  • Does it force me to create an account before I can use it?
  • Where is my data stored — on my device, or on their servers?
  • Does it show ads or include third-party trackers and analytics?
  • Can I delete everything easily, and does deleting actually remove it?

If the answers are vague, that's an answer.
